How It Works

From First Sale to Smarter Prediction.

Eight simple steps take a vendor from raw sales data to a fully optimized, waste-reducing operation.

1

Enter or Import Sales Data

Vendor enters or imports daily sales information into Israf Guard.

2

Analyze Historical Patterns

Israf Guard analyzes historical data — day of week, trends, and past leftovers.

3

Predict Expected Demand

The system forecasts how many meals should be prepared tomorrow.

4

Recommend Preparation Quantity

Vendor receives a clear, confident, recommended preparation quantity.

5

Record Daily Leftovers

Prepared, sold, remaining, and wasted quantities are logged at close.

6

Learn & Improve

The system learns from every day and improves tomorrow's forecast.

7

Offer Surplus at a Discount

Near closing time, surplus food becomes a limited-time student offer.

8

Redirect What Remains

Unsold-but-safe food goes to donation; unsuitable food goes to compost.
